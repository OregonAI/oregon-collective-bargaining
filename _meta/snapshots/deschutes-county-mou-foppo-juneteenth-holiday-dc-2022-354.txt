                          -- Recognition of Paid Juneteenth Holiday --
                                  Document Number: 2022-354

   Deschutes County, hereinafter referred to as “the County,” and the Federation of Oregon Parole &
   Probation Officers, hereinafter referred to as “the Federation,” agree to the following modification of
   Article 18 of the Collective Bargaining Agreement between the County and the Federation, dated
   October 17, 2018 ("CBA"), effective immediately upon execution of this Memorandum of Understanding
   ("MOU"):

   ARTICLE 18 - HOLIDAYS, Section 1 will be amended as follows (amendment in bold italics):

   Section |
   The following shall be recognized as paid holidays:
                            New Year’s Day                                    Labor Day
                            President’s Day                                   Veteran’s Day
                            Martin Luther King’s Day                          Thanksgiving Day
                            Memorial Day                                      Christmas Day
                            Juneteenth                                        Two (2) Floating Holidays
                            Independence Day

   Whenever a holiday falls on Sunday, the following Monday shall be considered a holiday. If a holiday falls
   on Saturday, the preceding Friday shall be a holiday. If an employee works on the actual holiday that falls on
   a Saturday or Sunday, that day would be the employee’s holiday and he/she will be paid in accordance with
   Article 18, Section 4 for that day only. In no event shall an employee receive holiday pay for both the
   observed and actual holiday. Holidays that occur during paid vacation or sick leave shall not be charged
   against vacation or sick leave. In order to qualify for holiday pay, employees must work their normal
   workdays before and after the holiday or be on authorized leave of absence with pay.

   Any dispute concerning this agreement will be resolved under the terms of Article 7 - Grievance
   Procedure, of the CBA.

    Except as specifically modified by thls MOU, all terms and conditions of the CBA shall remain in full
   force and effect. To the extent any of the terms of this MOU conflict with those in the CBA, the i
   and conditions of this MOU shall prevail for so long as it is in effect.
