                                         LETTER OF AGREEMENT
                  REGARDING ASSIGNMENT OF PROJECTS TO CAPITAL PROJECT SERVICES
                                     DIVISION INSPECTORS
                                                     Between
                                             WASHINGTON COUNTY

                                             TEAMSTER LOCAL #223


Washington County (“County”) and Teamsters Local #223 (“Union”) are parties to a Collective Bargaining
Agreement (“CBA”) currently in effect through June 30, 2026. County and Union are collectively referred to as
“Parties” below. This Letter of Agreement (LOA) is meant to apply only to Capital Project Services (CPS)
Division projects.

Background

Management reserves the right to assign work based on project needs, staff availability, the need for project
continuity, and other project-related factors. The Capital Project Services Division’s (CPS) projects tend to be
of relatively long duration and may take multiple years to complete. Shifting inspectors from project to project
during construction is disruptive, inefficient and creates opportunities for inconsistency. It has been a common
practice that the assigned Inspector stay on a project until the project is completed.

CBA Article 7.4.3. refers to shift assignments rather than work assignments and is therefore not applicable to
this specific issue. However, CPS management agrees that project assignments could be collaborative and
involve discussions with the Teamster Inspectors.

Agreement
The Capital Project Services Division (CPS) does project inspection staffing projections at least twice a year.
During the project/contract development process, Inspectors (Engineering Technicians) are assigned projects
based on their availability, experience, training needs and other factors determined by management.

Once the scope and scale of project work has been identified for upcoming projects, CPS management will
share with their Inspection staff their initial project assignment determination. Inspectors will have the
opportunity to provide input and express their interest in working on specific future projects.

To facilitate interactive dialogue, twice a year, a “Project Assignment Interest” email will be sent to all CPS
Inspectors. If an inspector has an interest in a particular project, they may email their inspection supervisor the
order of preference for the various projects being constructed that they would like to work on. The Inspector
expressions of interest will be considered in a discussion between the Division Manager, Principal Project
Managers and Inspection Supervisors to make a final determination of which Inspectors will be assigned to
each of the projects. Some of the factors used to decide this will include the Inspector’s availability,
experience, training needs and expressions of interest in a particular project.
The final management determination regarding CPS Inspector assignments will be provided in writing to the
Inspectors who expressed an interest in a particular project, including the rationale for project assignments.
This LOA shall become effective when the agreement is signed by all parties.

Except as modified above, all other provisions of the applicable CBA shall continue to apply.

Any disputes regarding the interpretation or application of this LOA shall be resolved by the Parties using the
grievance procedures of the applicable CBA.

In addition to the applicable CBA, this LOA is the full and final agreement of the Parties regarding this issue.
