                                                                    I. Parties

The parties to this Memorandum of Understanding are Washington County, a political subdivision of the State of
Oregon (“County”), and Washington County Police Officers’ Association Enforcement/Corrections Unit (WCPOA
ECU) and Washington County Police Officers’ Association Sheriff's Support Group (WCPOA SSG) (hereinafter
“parties”).

                                                                 Il. Background

The parties are currently subject to collective bargaining agreements (CBAs) in effect through June 30, 2025. The
parties recently discovered an oversight regarding Time Off In Lieu of Holidays in paragraph 16.1 of the WCPOA
ECU and paragraph 16.1(A) of the WCPOA SSG CBAs.

                                                                 Ill. Agreement

1.    After discussion and opportunity to negotiate, the parties mutually agree that as an addendum to the above
      referenced CBA paragraphs 16.1 and 16.1(A), going forward, each member shall, at the commencement of
      each fiscal year, beginning July 1, 2023, be accorded one (1) additional In Lieu of Holiday day (eight (8) hours).

2.    This MOU shall become effective when the agreement is signed by all parties.

3.    For the current fiscal year (2023/2024), the additional eight (8) hours of In Lieu of Holiday accruals shall be
      added to qualifying members’ accruals at the next pay period after the agreement becomes effective.

4.    Except as modified above, all other provisions of the applicable CBA shall continue to apply.

5.    Any disputes regarding the interpretation or application of this MOU shall be resolved by the Parties using the
      grievance procedures of the applicable CBA.

6.    In addition to the applicable CBA, this MOU           is the full and final agreement of the Parties regarding this issue.
