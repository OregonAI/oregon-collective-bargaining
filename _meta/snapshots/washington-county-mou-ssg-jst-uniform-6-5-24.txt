                                     Memorandum of Understanding (MOU)

                                                            Between

                                             Washington County (County)

                                                               And

             Washington County Police Officers’ Association — Sheriff's Support Group (WCPOA-SSG)


                                                             Parties

The parties to this MOU are the Washington County Sheriff’s Office (WCSO), and the WCPOA-SSG.



                                                          Background                                  i
The parties are currently subject to a Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA) in effect through June 30,
2025.

The subject of this MOU is Article 15.1 A. of the SSG CBA. It was recently discovered that some JSTs were
being asked to purchase their own serviceable uniform pants if the County issued pants did not fit
correctly. The intent of this MOU is to remove the requirement for JSTs to purchase uniform items with
their own money if they have been approved by the Uniform Committee.



    1.   The parties agree that Article 15.1.A.3 in the SSG contract will now read:

Except as otherwise set forth in Article 15 of this CBA, the parties will adhere to the JST Dress Code
Guidelines. items approved by the WCSO Uniform Committee will be purchased for or reimbursed to JSTs
as approved. Items not approved by the Uniform Committee may be approved by the Division
Commander to be purchased at the requesting employee’s own expense.
    2.   This MOU shall become effective when signed by the Parties.

    3.   Except as modified above, all other provisions of the CBA shall continue to apply.

    4.   Any disputes regarding the interpretation or application of this MOU shall be resolved by the
         Parties using the grievance procedures of the CBA.

    5.   In addition to the CBA, this MOU is the full and final agreement of the Parties regarding this issue.
