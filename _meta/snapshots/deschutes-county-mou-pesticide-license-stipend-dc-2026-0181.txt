   Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) between Deschutes County and American Federation
              of State, County and Municipal Employees Local 3997 (AFSCME)
                            Regarding Pesticide License Stipend
                            Original Effective Date: April 1, 2026

                                     Document #DC-2026-0181

This Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") is entered into by and between Deschutes County (the
"County") and the American Federation of State, County and Municipal Employees Local 3997
("AFSCME"). The County and AFSCME are parties to the Agreement between Deschutes County and the
American Federation of State, County and Municipal Employees Local 3997 ("Agreement").
The purpose of this MOU is for the County and the AFSCME to come to an agreement to provide the
County with the ability to implement a recurring monthly stipend for eligible represented employees
who obtain and maintain a current and approved Pesticide Applicator License via the Oregon
Department of Agriculture (ODA).
1. Parties and Purpose
   A. This Memorandum of Understanding (“MOU”) is entered into by and between Deschutes County
      (“County”) and the American Federation of State, County and Municipal Employees (AFSCME)
      Council 75, on behalf of those AFSCME-represented employees who are eligible for the stipend
      for obtaining and maintaining an ODA approved Pesticide Applicator License as contemplated
      herein.
   B. The County, in its sole discretion, shall select maintenance positions eligible to receive this
      stipend subject to availability of funds. The County may expand or contract this stipend as
      allowed under Federal, State, County laws and/or policies. Nothing in this MOU shall preclude
      the County from making changes to the incentive programs detailed below, including
      discontinuing the programs for any reason.
   C. The Association acknowledges that under ORS 243.672{1)(e) the parties are obligated to bargain
      in good faith prior to altering the status quo of any mandatory subject of bargaining that is not
      included in the CBA. The Association hereby waives any right under federal, state, or common
      law to bargain any aspect of the incentive program detailed in this MOU, including the
      implementation, modification, or discontinuation of the program.
   D. Pursuant to this MOU, the County may, in its sole discretion, implement the following stipend.

2. Effective Date and Duration
   A. Effective Date: April 1, 2026.
   B. This MOU remains in effect from April 1, 2026 until modified by the parties, rescinded by mutual
      agreement, or incorporated into a successor collective bargaining agreement (CBA).


3. Definitions
   A.   “Eligible License”: The ODA Pesticide Applicator license as specified by Department Leadership
   B.   “Eligible Employee”: Any AFSCME-represented employee of the County who holds an active,
        eligible License and meets the verification requirements in Section 6 of this MOU.


4. Stipend Amount and Payment Method
   A. Amount: $160.00 per month.
          i.   FTE Pro-ration: The stipend is pro-rated by the employee’s budgeted FTE (e.g., 1.0 FTE =
               $160; 0.75 FTE = $120; 0.5 FTE = $80).
   B. Pay Period Distribution: The monthly amount (or pro-rated amount) will be paid beginning the
      first full pay period after HR verifies eligibility as defined by section 6 of this MOU.
   C. Partial Month/Leaves: For months in which an employee is in a paid status for only part of the
      month (e.g., mid-month onboarding, unpaid leave), the stipend is pro-rated by the portion of the
      month in paid status.


5. Interaction with Other Premiums and Differentials
   A. Stacking: This stipend may be received concurrently with other approved premiums/differentials
      (e.g., bilingual, certification/skill pay, hazard, water operator), unless a specific CBA or MOU
      expressly makes another premium mutually exclusive.
   B. No Pyramiding: The stipend is a stand-alone premium and does not multiply or pyramid with
      other pays except as required by law for overtime regular-rate calculations (see Section 10 of this
      MOU).


6. Eligibility Verification and Administration
   A. Employee Responsibility: To be eligible for the stipend, the employee must provide proof of an
      active Oregon Department of Agriculture (“ODA”) Pesticide Applicator license (e.g., copy of
      license card or ODA confirmation) to HR, the designated Department HR liaison, or the
      employee’s direct supervisor for submission to HR. The employee must also provide updated
 Document #DC-2026-0181                                                                   Page 2 | 6
       proof for each renewal of the license and for each new/renewed Stipend Agreement term, as
       applicable.
   B. County Verification: HR (or designee) will verify the active status of the license using ODA
      documentation or ODA’s verification tools and will record the license number and expiration
      date for payroll administration.
   C. Renewal to remain eligible:
         i.    License Renewal: The employee must renew before expiration and provide proof to HR
               no later than the effective expiration date.
        ii.    Stipend Term Renewal: Regardless of license expiration date, stipend continuation is not
               automatic. To continue receiving the stipend for the next Annual Stipend Term (April 1–
               March 31), the employee must have both:
                  a. an active eligible License; and
                  b. a newly executed Incentive Agreement covering that Annual Stipend Term.
        iii.   Late renewal / no retroactivity: County HR or County Leadership may establish internal
               deadlines to ensure processing prior to April 1st; failure to meet internal deadlines may
               result in a lapse of stipend payment. If the employee fails to provide proof of renewal and
               the stipend stops, stipend payments (if later approved) will resume no earlier than the
               first full pay period after HR receives acceptable proof and verifies eligibility, unless the
               County explicitly authorizes retroactive payment in writing.
   D. Start/Stop Triggers:
         i.    Start, the stipend begins the first full pay period following HR verification of an active
               Eligible License and execution of an Incentive Agreement covering the applicable Stipend
               Term or April 1st annually.
        ii.    Stop, the stipend stops on the earliest of any of the following:
                  a. License status change: At the end of the pay period in which the license expires,
                     lapses, is suspended, is revoked, or otherwise ceases to be active; or
                  b. End of Stipend Term: At the end of the pay period that includes March 31st (end of
                     Incentive Agreement’s Stipend Term), unless a new Stipend Agreement for the
                     next Annual Stipend Term has been fully executed and eligibility has verified
                     continuation; or

                  c. Loss of eligibility/position change: Effective as otherwise provided by the
                     MOU/program rules if the employee no longer meets the position-based eligibility
                     criteria designated by the County.


7. County Payment of Initial Licensing Costs (and One Retake)

   A. Covered Costs: For positions or assignments for which the County requires the ODA Pesticide
      license, the County will pay:
          i.    the ODA license application fee and initial exam fee; and
          ii.   the fee for one (1) exam retake if the employee does not pass on the first attempt.
         iii.   License renewal fees, prior to their expiration
        Optional training, materials and travel are reimbursable only if pre-approved and processed
        under the County’s training/travel policy.
   B. Payment Method: Costs may be paid directly by the County where feasible; otherwise
      reimbursed to the employee upon submission of itemized proof of payment within sixty (60)
      days, consistent with County reimbursement procedures. These are not wages and will be
      processed as business reimbursements in accordance with applicable law and County policy.
   C. Completion Deadline: Employees must obtain the Eligible License within twelve (12) months of
      (a) hire date into a position requiring the license, or (b) assignment/reclassification into a
      position for which the license is required - whichever provides the employee with the most time.
   D. Retake Limit: The County will cover at most one (1) retake fee within the same twelve-month
      window. Additional attempts are at the employee’s expense unless otherwise authorized in
      writing by the Department and HR.
   E. Failure to Obtain: Failure to obtain the Eligible License within the twelve-month period may
      affect the employee’s continued eligibility for the assignment/position and is subject to
      applicable CBA provisions and County policies. The stipend is not payable until licensure is
      obtained and verified per Section 7 and an incentive compensation agreement is executed by the
      employee seeking to be paid the stipend.
   F. Re-certification Costs: Recertification fees (when a license has lapsed due to expiration) are not
      covered by this Section unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.
   G. Scheduling/Compensation for Time: Exam and license activities should be scheduled, where
      operationally feasible, during regular work hours with supervisory approval. Time spent during
      scheduled work hours is compensable; time outside scheduled hours is not compensable unless
      pre-approved as work time.


8. Loss of License; Assignment Impact
   A.   Immediate Cessation: If an employee loses, allows to lapse, or fails to maintain the Eligible
        License, the stipend ceases as provided in Section 6.
   B.   Notice Obligation: The employee must notify their supervisor and HR in writing within five (5)
        business days of any change in license status.

   C.   Assignment/Position Requirements: Where a position, program, or assignment requires an active
        ODA Pesticide Applicator license to perform essential or regular duties, failure to maintain the
        license may affect the employee’s ability to continue in that assignment/position. The County will
        follow applicable CBA provisions and policies regarding reassignment, qualification standards,
        and any applicable interactive process obligations.


8. Scope of Use
   A.   This stipend recognizes the qualification and availability to lawfully perform pesticide-related
        duties for the County. It does not, by itself, require assignment of such duties. Assignment
        remains a management right subject to applicable CBA provisions.


9. FLSA, Wage, Tax, and Retirement Treatment
   A.   Regular Rate: The parties agree the stipend is non-discretionary compensation tied to
        qualifications and shall be included in the employee’s regular rate of pay for overtime calculation
        purposes under the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) and applicable Oregon wage law.
   B.   Taxable Compensation: The stipend is taxable income and subject to applicable withholdings.
   C.   PERS Reportability: The parties intend this stipend to be treated as remuneration for services.
        The County will administer reporting consistent with PERS rules and guidance then in effect. If
        PERS determines any different treatment, the County will adjust prospectively to comply with
        that determination.


10. No Retroactivity
   A.   Payments under this MOU will not be made for periods prior to April 1, 2026.


11. No Precedent; Severability; Entire Agreement on Subject
   A.   No Precedent: This MOU resolves a specific compensation item and does not establish a
        precedent for other premiums or differentials.
   B.   Severability: If any provision is found unlawful or unenforceable, the remainder of the MOU
        remains in effect, and the parties will bargain replacement language to the extent required by
        law.
   C.   Entire Agreement on Subject: This MOU constitutes the full agreement of the parties on the
        subject of a pesticide applicator license stipend and supersedes any contrary past practice. If
        there is a conflict between the CBA and this MOU on this specific subject, this MOU governs.
