Washington County and the Washington County Police Officers’ Association                  (WCPOA)      are parties to a collective
bargaining agreement (CBA) currently in effect through June 30, 2025.

This agreement, entered into on the date below written by and between the Washington County Police Officers’
Association (‘WCPOA or ‘Association’) and Washington County (the ‘County’), is for the purpose of amending Article
19 — Other Leaves, specifically section 19.4 Military or Peace Corps Leave.

                                                           ll. Background

Oregon State Legislature recently passed an amendment to Oregon House Bill 2865 (HB2865),                      increasing military
leave of absence entitled for public employees for initial active duty for training and annual active-duty training, effective
January 1, 2024.


HB2865 increases the military leave of absence for public employees who are members of the National Guard,
National Guard Reserve, or reserve components of the United States Armed Forces or United States Public Health
Service to 21 work days.
