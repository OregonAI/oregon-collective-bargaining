                                 MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
                    REGARDING APPLICATION OF OVERTIME DUE TO SHIFT CHANGE IN THE
                                      CAPITAL PROJECT SERVICES DIVISION


                                                 Between
                                             WASHINGTON COUNTY
                                                    And
                                             TEAMSTER LOCAL #223

Washington County (“County”) and Teamsters Local #223 (“Union”) are parties to a Collective Bargaining
Agreement (“CBA”) currently in effect through June 30, 2026. County and Union are collectively referred to as
“Parties” below. This Letter of Agreement (LOA) is meant to apply only to Capital Project Services (CPS)
Divisions’ projects.

Background
Due to the nature of their work, each division in Land Use & Transportation (LUT) has unique work processes,
procedures and staffing needs. In the Capital Project Services Division (CPS), construction contractors often
decide their work schedule with just a day or two notice. Often notice is given solely to the Teamster Inspector
on the project site. In some cases, the supervisor or division leadership may determine that an Inspector shift
change is not necessary, depending on the reason for the requested change. For example, if consulted
beforehand, the supervisor may consider a sidewalk pour or traffic control set up as not requiring an Inspector
shift change, whereas a bridge deck pour would likely be approved.

Agreement
Washington County and the Teamsters Local agree to clarify implementation of Article 7.4.1 and 7.4.2 for the
Capital Project Services Division (CPS) as follows.

For a shift change of less than or equal to one (1) hour outside of a regularly scheduled shift, no approval from
the Inspector’s supervisor is needed. The employee will not be compensated with the guaranteed four (4)
hours of overtime in this instance (Article 7.4.2).

The Inspector will be compensated with overtime for any hours worked outside the regularly scheduled shift.

CPS will have all Teamster employees compensated at the overtime rate for the first four (4) hours of the first
day of the rescheduled shift whenever their shift is changed for an amount of time greater than one (1) hour
outside of their regularly scheduled shift without 7 days’ notice with the following conditions:

   -   If the Inspector shift change is initiated and directed by the Supervisor.

   -   When the request for shift change is not initiated by the Supervisor, CPS requires that the Teamster
       Inspector check in with the Supervisor about the need to change their shift. If the Supervisor agrees
       that a shift change is needed, the Inspector may ask for the shift change and be compensated at the
       overtime rate for the first four (4) hours of the first day of the rescheduled shift.


This LOA shall become effective when the agreement is signed by all parties.
Except as modified above, all other provisions of the applicable CBA shall continue to apply.

Any disputes regarding the interpretation or application of this LOA shall be resolved by the Parties using the
grievance procedures of the applicable CBA.

In addition to the applicable CBA, this LOA is the full and final agreement of the Parties regarding this issue.
