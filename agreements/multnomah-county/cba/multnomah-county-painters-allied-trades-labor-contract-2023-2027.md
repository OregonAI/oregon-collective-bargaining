15         This Agreement is entered into by Multnomah County, Oregon, hereinafter
16   referred to as the County, and International Union of Painters and Allied Trades
17   and District Council 5, AFL-CIO, Local 1094 hereinafter referred to as the “Union.”
18         The purpose of this Agreement is to set forth those matters pertaining to
19   rates of pay, hours of work, fringe benefits, and other matters pertaining to
20   employment consistent with the County's objective of providing ever improved
21   services to the public of Multnomah County.
22         The parties agree as follows:

 1                                            ARTICLE 2
 2                                           DEFINITIONS
 3
 4   1.     Cause
 5          Misconduct,      inefficiency,    incompetence,   insubordination,    indolence,
 6   malfeasance, unfitness to render effective service or failing to fulfill responsibilities
 7   as an employee.
 8   2.     Continuous Service
 9          Means uninterrupted employment with Multnomah County subject to the
10   following provisions:
11          A.        Continuous service shall include uninterrupted employment with
12   another governmental agency accomplished in accordance with and subject to
13   ORS 236.605 through 236.640.
14           B.    Continuous service is terminated by voluntary termination, unless the
15   employee returns to County service within twelve (12) months, involuntary
16   termination due to expiration of a layoff list, or discharge for cause.
17   3.     Initial Trial Service Employee
18          A regular employee serving a one (1) year period of trial service to
19   determine their suitability for continued employment, such period to begin on the
20   date of their appointment to a regular position from a certified list of eligibles.
21   During the period of probation, the employee may be dismissed without recourse
22   to the grievance procedure if, in the opinion of the employee’s manager, their
23   continued service would not be in the best interest of the County. The length of an
24   employee’s initial trial service period may not be extended by a Memorandum of
25   Agreement under the terms of Article 22, Entire Agreement, unless the employee
26   was absent from work for a period of forty-five (45) days or more previous to the
27   extension.
28   4.     Managerial Employee
29          Means any individual having authority in the interest of the employer to hire,
30   transfer, suspend, layoff, recall, promote, discharge, assign, reward, or discipline
31   other employees, or having responsibility to direct them, adjust their grievances,
32   or effectively to recommend such action, if in connection therewith, the exercise of


                                   ARTICLE 2, DEFINITIONS
                                                                                         -3-


 1   such authority is not of a merely routine or clerical nature, but requires the use of
 2   independent judgment.
 3   5.     Promotional Trial Service Employee
 4          A regular employee serving a six (6) month period of trial service upon
 5   promotion to determine their suitability for continued employment in the job profile
 6   to which the employee was promoted, such period to begin on the date of their
 7   appointment to a higher job profile from a certified list of eligibles. During the period
 8   of promotional trial service, the employee shall be returned to the job profile and
 9   department from which the employee was promoted without recourse to the
10   grievance procedure if, in the opinion of the employee’s manager, their continued
11   service in the job profile to which they were promoted would not be in the best
12   interest of the County.
13   6.     Regular Employee
14          An employee who has passed the initial trial service period in effect at the
15   time of their appointment, and has been employed by the County continuously
16   since passing the initial trial service period. In addition, the following are deemed
17   to be regular employees:
18          A.     An employee who has passed the initial one (1) year trial service
19   period, terminated employment, and has been reinstated.
20          B.     A non-initial trial service employee who has been transferred to the
21   County by intergovernmental agreement under ORS 236.605 through 236.640.
22   7.     Temporary Employee
23          A person employed to meet short term, non-recurring or emergency County
24   work needs.

 4   1.     Unit Definition
 5          The County recognizes the Union as the sole and exclusive bargaining
 6   agent for all non-supervisory employee members of the bargaining unit for the
 7   purpose of establishing salaries, wages, hours, and other conditions of
 8   employment. The job profile covered by this Agreement are listed in Addendum A
 9   attached hereto and made a part hereof.
10   2.     On-Call Employees
11          It is agreed that on-call Sign Fabricator are covered by the parties’ collective
12   bargaining agreement. On-call Sign Fabricators are defined as an employee
13   employed to meet intermittent, irregular, or less than half-time county work needs,
14   and may be terminated at any time and have no appeal rights within the County.
15          On-call employees shall be entitled to only the following contractual
16   benefits:
17          A.     Union Security, Check Off and Business. (Article 5)
18          B.     Holiday compensation at one and one-half (1 ½) times the normal
19   hourly wage for work on anyone of the following holidays (Article 7.1):

 4   1.     Monthly Listing of New and Terminated Employees
 5          The County agrees to furnish the Union, each month, a listing of all new
 6   employees covered by this Agreement hired during the month and of all employees
 7   who terminated during the month. Such listing shall contain the names of the
 8   employees, along with their job profiles, work locations, and home addresses.
 9   2.     Union Security and Check-Off
10          The County agrees to deduct each pay period from the pay of employees
11   covered by this Agreement as applicable:
12          A.     One-half (.5) of the current monthly Union membership dues of those
13   Union members who individually request such deductions in writing on the form
14   provided by the Union and submitted to Multnomah County Central Payroll.
15          B.     The amounts to be deducted shall be certified to the County by the
16   Financial Secretary of the Union, and the aggregate of all deductions shall be
17   remitted, together with an itemized statement to the Treasurer of the Union by the
18   first day of the succeeding month after such deductions are made.
19          C.     The Union will indemnify, defend and hold the County harmless from
20   claims arising out of application of this article for any amount of any unauthorized
21   deduction resulting from the County’s reliance on any Union provided list of
22   authorized dues payment.

28   labor/management committees, Benefits Committee, Compensation Committee;
29   duties as a Steward as defined in this agreement and such other Union Business
30   (County Paid Time) that are mutually agreed between the parties. County

 1   employees participating in such activities will be allowed to do so without loss of

 3           B.    Union Business Leave (Union Reimbursable Time)
 4                 Any bargaining unit member selected by the Union to participate in a
 5   Union activity shall be considered on Union Business Leave (Union Reimbursable
 6   Time) status and shall be granted such paid leave without loss of pay.
 7                 The Union agrees to reimburse the County one-hundred percent
 8   (100%) of the affected employee’s salary and fringe benefits (including pro-rata
 9   cost of workers compensation premiums, but excluding indirect administration or
10   overhead charges) for straight time spent on Union activities conducted during
11   regularly scheduled working hours. The County shall submit a monthly statement
12   to the Union itemizing the amount of the Union’s reimbursement obligation, and
13   the Union will reimburse the County within thirty (30) days of receipt of the monthly
14   union reimbursable time statement.
15                 Union Business (Union Reimbursable Time) addressed in this
16   section would pertain to such activities as contract administration, time to cover for
17   staff   replacement,    time    to   attend    training   conferences      such     as
18   steward/arbitration/grievance training; and time off to prepare for negotiations.
19                 Written requests of such time away from work shall be given to the
20   affected employee’s immediate manager five (5) working days in advance and
21   shall be approved subject to County operational and business needs. The Union
22   will make every effort to avoid disruptions of work.
23           C.    Union Business Leave - Employment Status
24                 Employees in Union Business Leave County Paid time and Union
25   Reimbursable time shall be treated as in paid leave status regarding accrual of
26   benefits such as vacation, sick leave, Health and Welfare, pension or any other
27   benefit granted employees in paid leave status.
28                 During Union Reimbursable Time, the employee shall not be eligible
29   for County workers’ compensation benefits arising out of an injury or illness
30   occurring during the leave from the County.

 1                                       ARTICLE 6
 2                                       NO STRIKE
 3
 4          No employee covered by this Agreement shall engage in any work
 5   stoppage, slowdown, picketing, or strike at any County facility or at any location in
 6   the County where County services are performed during the life and duration of
 7   this Agreement. If any such work stoppage, slowdown, picketing, or strike shall
 8   take place, the Union will immediately notify such employees so engaging in such
 9   activities to cease and desist, and it shall publicly declare that such work stoppage,
10   slowdown, picketing, or strike is in violation of this Agreement and is unauthorized.
11   Employees in the bargaining unit, while acting in the course of their employment,
12   shall not refuse to cross any picket line established in the County by any labor
13   organization when called upon to cross such picket line in the line of duty. Any
14   employee engaging in any activity in violation of this article shall be subject to
15   immediate disciplinary action or discharge. It is understood, however, that no
16   employee shall be disciplined or discharged for refusal to cross the picket line
17   when directed to perform work which does not properly fall within the scope and
18   jurisdiction of this local Union.

 4   1.     Grievance Procedure
 5          Any grievance or dispute which may arise between the parties, involving the
 6   application, meaning, or interpretation of this Agreement, shall be settled in the following
 7   manner:
 8          A.     Step I - The Immediate Manager
 9                 After first attempting to resolve the grievance informally, any employee or
10   the Union may present in writing such grievance to the employee's section or division
11   head through the immediate manager within ten (10) working days of its occurrence; if at
12   that time the individual employee or their representative is unaware of the grievance, it
13   may be presented in writing within ten (10) working days of the time the employee first
14   has knowledge or should have had knowledge of its occurrence. The notice shall include
15   a statement of the grievance and relevant facts, applicable provisions of the contract, and
16   remedies sought. The manager shall then attempt to adjust the matter and respond, in
17   writing, to the employee or their representative within ten (10) working days.
18          B.     Step II - The Department Director
19                 If the grievance has not been answered or resolved, it may be presented in
20   writing by the employee or their representative to the Department Director within
21   ten (10) working days after the response is due from the manager. The Department
22   Director shall respond to the employee or their representative, in writing, within
23   ten (10) working days.
24          C.     Step III - Labor Relations
25                 If the grievance has not been answered or resolved at Step II, it may be
26   presented, in writing, by the grievant to Labor Relations, within ten (10) working days after
27   the response of the Department Director is due. Labor Relations shall respond in writing
28   to the grievant within ten (10) working days.
29          D.     County Grievances
30                 When the County has a grievance, it may be presented in writing to the
31   Union through the Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO) in Central Human Resources



                           ARTICLE 17, SETTLEMENT OF DISPUTES
     - 52 -


 1   or their representative. The parties will each then promptly appoint two (2) persons to
 2   serve as a Board of Adjustment to consider the grievance of the County and resolve the
 3   dispute. If the Board of Adjustment is unable to resolve the dispute within ten (10) days
 4   of the notification to the Union, then the County may request arbitration under Step V of
 5   this Grievance Procedure, by written notice to the other party. This procedure for County
 6   grievances is not exclusive and the County expressly retains the right to alternately
 7   proceed with any other action, including court proceedings, it may deem in its discretion
 8   to be advisable or warranted.
 9            E.   Step IV - Request for Arbitration
10                 If the grievance has not been answered or resolved at Step III, either party
11   may, within ten (10) working days after the expiration of the time limit specified in Step III,
12   request arbitration by written notice to the other party.
13            F.   Step V - Arbitration
14                 After the grievance has been submitted to arbitration, the parties, or their
15   representatives, shall jointly request the Oregon Mediation and Conciliation Service for a
16   list of the names of seven (7) arbitrators. The parties shall select an arbitrator from the list
17   by mutual agreement. If the parties are unable to agree on a method, the arbitrator will
18   be chosen by the method of alternate striking of names; the order of striking to be

22                 The arbitrator shall be requested to begin taking evidence and testimony
23   within a reasonable period after submission of the request for arbitration taking into
24   account the schedules of the parties' representatives, the arbitrator, and witnesses; and

27   authority to compel the attendance of witnesses on behalf of either party by issuance of
28   a subpoena, the cost of which shall be borne by the party requesting the subpoena.

30   power to alter, modify, amend, add to, or detract from the terms of the Contract. The

17
18   B.   In the MOA, language was included regarding operationally essential employee
19        designation and compensation. All IUPAT Local 1094 employees are designated
20        as “Operationally Essential” and required to report for duty regardless of facility
21        closure or curtailment of some or all County operations and receive the Operationally
22        Essential Assignment Compensation, which is two (2) saved holidays to be used
23        within the fiscal year.
24
25   C.   IUPAT Local 1094 employees were designated as “Operationally Essential”
26        employees in the MOA that was agreed to by the parties to extend the 2016-2021
27        CBA through June 30, 2023. Operationally Essential Employees are required to
28        report to work when the County closes or curtails operations. However, they are not
29        subject to the “Emergency Conditions Provisions” when the Director of the
30        Department of Community Services (hereinafter referred to as “DCS”) designates
31        an emergency. An “Emergency” is defined as inclement weather or other condition,

 1        which in the sole discretion of the Director of DCS, or their designee, constitutes a
 2        present or imminent danger to the health, safety, or property of the people of
 3        Multnomah County.

 5   D.   The Road Maintenance section of DCS has a need for volunteers when the Director
 6        of the Department of Community Services designates an “Emergency” as defined
 7        above in Section II.C.

27             II. Agreement
28
29                A.   An “Emergency” is defined as inclement weather or other condition,
30                     which in the sole discretion of the Director of Community Services or

 2          safety, or property of the people of Multnomah County.

 4     B.   During the term of such an emergency, the "work day" for pay
 5          purposes shall be the calendar day (midnight to midnight); however,
 6          the Department will not schedule shifts overlapping calendar days for
 7          the purpose of avoiding overtime pay.
 8
 9     C.   An employee sent home during the work day, regardless of whether or
10          not the employee is recalled, shall receive a minimum of eight (8) hours
11          of pay for that work day.
12
13     D.   The total number of hours worked during the work day, regardless of
14          how divided, shall be added to determine the total number of hours
15          worked for pay purposes during the work day.
16
17     E.   All hours worked in excess of the employee’s regular scheduled hours
18          for the day shall be compensated at the overtime rate of pay. For
19          example, an employee regularly scheduled to work a 4-10 schedule
20          will receive overtime after ten (10) hours in the workday. However, on
21          the first (1st) day of the emergency, any employee sent home and
22          called back within the same work day shall receive an additional two
23          (2) hours of overtime pay in addition to the compensation as computed
24          and paid as the paragraph above.
25
26     F.   All hours worked during swing and graveyard shifts shall be paid at the
27          contractually required shift differential.
28
29     G.   In recognition of the difficult conditions required during emergencies

31          scheduling work, employees will receive a twenty percent (20%)
