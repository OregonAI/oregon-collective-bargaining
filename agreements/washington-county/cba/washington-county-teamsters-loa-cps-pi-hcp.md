                                           LETTER OF AGREEMENT
                REGARDING HIGHER CLASS PAY ASSIGNMENTS FOR CAPITAL PROJECT SERVICES
                                        DIVISION INSPECTORS
                                                     Between
                                             WASHINGTON COUNTY
                                                    And
                                             TEAMSTER LOCAL #223

Washington County (“County”) and Teamsters Local #223 (“Union”) are parties to a Collective Bargaining
Agreement (“CBA”) currently in effect through June 30, 2026. County and Union are collectively referred to as
“Parties” below. This Letter of Agreement (LOA) is meant to apply only to Capital Project Services (CPS)
Divisions’ projects.


Background

This LOA relates to application of Article 11.1 regarding eligibility of Engineering Technician II Inspector
employees for Higher Class Pay assignment on CPS projects.

The Capital Project Services Divisions’ (CPS) projects vary significantly in size and complexity. An Engineering
Technician II may be assigned as the only Inspector on smaller or simpler projects. For larger and/or more
complex projects, an Engineering Technician III and an Engineering Technician II may both be assigned.

The Engineering Technician III is the advanced journey level classification within the Engineering Technician
series. As the primary project inspector on a larger and/or more complex project, the Engineering Technician
III will generally determine what work will be inspected by the secondary inspector (Engineering Technician II).
The Engineering Tech II is distinguished from the Engineering Tech III by still requiring guidance and direction
in certain situations. At no time will an Engineering Technician I be assigned as a primary Inspector in the
Capital Project Services Division (CPS).

Agreement
The parties agree to the following clarification regarding CBA Article 11.1 as applied to the Capital Project
Services Division (CPS) Inspectors. Article 11.1 generally requires the county to pay 5% above an employee’s
current base rate for any work for more than four (4) consecutive hours in a classification above that in which
the employee is classified. For clarification, higher class pay for CPS Engineering Technician II Inspectors will be
assigned as follows:

   -   Some CPS projects, due to their smaller size, scale, scope and/or limited complexity, are
       commensurate with an Engineering Technician II classification and scope of work. Higher class pay as
       discussed in Article 11.1 will not apply on these projects even when the Engineering Technician II is the
       sole inspector on the job. Such projects include, but are not limited to the following:

           ▪   ADA ramp projects, Gain Share pedestrian improvement projects, URMD pedestrian and bicycle
               improvement projects as well as half-street improvements.
           ▪   Signal replacements and/or upgrades, walls, waterline work, utility relocation.
           ▪   Culvert replacement projects.
           ▪   Trail projects and pedestrian bridges.
   -   For larger or more complex projects, an Engineering Technician III and an Engineering Technician II may
       concurrently be assigned to the project. In this situation, if the Engineering Technician III is absent from
       work for four (4) days or less, this will not trigger Article 11.1 because the Engineering Technician II will
       be expected to utilize the expertise of their assigned Inspection Supervisor, Principal Project Manager,
       and/or the Project Manager for the specific project they are assigned to in the absence of the
       Engineering Technician III.

   -   When an Engineering Technician III is absent for more than four days, and the Inspection Supervisor
       approves for the Engineering Technician II to assume the role of the primary inspector, Article 11.1
       shall apply.

This LOA shall become effective when the agreement is signed by all parties.

Except as modified above, all other provisions of the applicable CBA shall continue to apply.

Any disputes regarding the interpretation or application of this LOA shall be resolved by the Parties using the
grievance procedures of the applicable CBA.

In addition to the applicable CBA, this LOA is the full and final agreement of the Parties regarding this issue.


Effective as of the last signature date below.
