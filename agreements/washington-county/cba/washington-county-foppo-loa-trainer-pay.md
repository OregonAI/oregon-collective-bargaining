Washington County
And
Federation of Oregon Parole and Probation Officers
Letter of Agreement regarding Trainer Pay
Washington County and the Federation of Oregon Parole and Probation Officers agree
that training other PPOS is a classification requirement for those employees in the Parole and
Probation Officer III classification (PPO III). Therefore, the parties agree that employees in the
PPO III classification are not eligible for "Other Trainer Pay" as outlined in Section 12.3(b) of
the parties' collective bargaining agreement.
