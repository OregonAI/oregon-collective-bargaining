                           Memorandum of Understanding
                                       Between
                            Washington County ('County')
                                         And
        American Federation of State, County, Municipal Employees ("AFSCME")


                                           I. Parties
The parties to this Memorandum of Understanding are Washington County, a political
subdivision of the State of Oregon ("County"), and American Federation of State, County,
Municipal Employees ("AFSCME"), (hereinafter "parties").

                                           II. Background
The parties are currently subject to a collective bargaining agreement (CSA) in effect through
June 30, 2027.

This Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) is entered into for the purpose of conducting a
pilot program to test an alternative work schedule of four (4) ten-hour shifts per week ("4/10
schedule") in place of the current five (5) eight-hour shifts per week ("5/8 schedule") for
AFSCME represented, Full-Time, Community Correction Specialist Classifications.

                                           Ill. Agreement

1. The 4/10 pilot will begin on February 28, 2026 (the beginning of a pay period) and will
   conclude on August 28, 2026 (the end of a pay period).
2. At the conclusion of the pilot, employees will revert to the 5/8 schedule beginning on
   August 29, 2026, unless the parties mutually agree to extend, modify, or make permanent
   the 4/10 schedule.

3. This pilot applies to the classification of Corrections Specialists (I, II, and Ill) in entirety.

4. Shifts will be structured as Day: 6:00am - 4:30pm, Swing: 2:00pm - 12:30am, Night:
   10:00pm - 8:30am. The parties may mutually agree to adjust these shift times.

5. The parties agree that implementation of the 4/10 schedule shall modify the following
   provisions of the CBA solely for the duration of this MOU unless the duration is extended
   as outlined in "2. Duration."
       -Article 12.10.Lead/FloaterPay: During the 4/10 schedule, to maintain consistent
       Lead/Floater coverage without duplicate pay, the following will apply: Only one
       employee will receive Lead/Floater pay at a time; When shifts overlap, the outgoing
       shift's designated Lead/Floater will keep Lead/Floater pay until the end of their shift;
       Once the outgoing Lead/Floater departs, the incoming shift's designated Lead/Floater
       will begin receiving Lead/Floater pay. This arrangement may be reviewed and adjusted
       by mutual agreement during the pilot period.
      -Article 12.13 - Wages - Shift Differential: Shift differentials will be defined by
      assigned shift. For the 4/10 schedule: Employees working Day shift (eg. 6:00 am - 4:30
      pm) receive base pay; Employees working Swing shift (eg. 2:00 pm - 12:30 am) receive
      swing shift differential of one dollar ($1.00) per hour; Employees working Night shift (eg.
      10:00 pm - 8:30 am) receive night shift differential of one dollar and fifty cents ($1.50)
      per hour. Employees working full or partial shifts will be eligible for shift differential.

             -   Ex1: If someone from Dayshift is also covering a Swing shift (6:00 am - 10:00
                 pm), they will receive swing shift differential pay at 2:00 pm.
             -   Ex2: If someone from Swing shift is also covering a Night shift (2:00 pm - 6:00
                 am), they will begin receiving night shift differential pay at 10:00 pm.
             -   Ex3: If two staff split a Swing shift (Day shift staff: 6:00 am - 7:00 pm, Night
                 shift staff: 7:00 pm - 8:00 am), the Day shift employee begins receiving Swing
                 shift differential at 2:00 pm, the Night shift employee begins receiving Swing
                 shift differential at 7:00 pm and then begins receiving Night shift differential at
                 10:00 pm.

      -Article 18.3 - Overtime - Distribution: An employee may not work more than two shifts
      (sixteen (16) hours) in a twenty-four-hour period or three shifts (twenty-six (26) hours) in
      a forty-eight hour period.

      -Article 19.3 - Vacation Leave - Vacation Schedule: During the six-month pilot
      transition from a 5/8 schedule to a 4/10 schedule, employees will be provided the
      opportunity to adjust previously approved vacation bid days to align with their new work
      schedules. This process is consistent with current practice when employees move to a
      different shift/schedule. Such adjustments shall not exceed the originally approved total
      of ten (10) vacation bid days for the bid year, subject to the employee's accrued vacation
      and/or comp banks. In the event the MOU duration is extended as outlined in "2.
      Duration," the parties will review the annual vacation bid process for any necessary
      adjustments.

6. Data Collection & Evaluation: The parties agree to monitor and evaluate the pilot using the
following measures:
         • Overtime hours.
         • Sick leave use.
         • Staffing and coverage issues.
         • Related operational costs.
         • Employee feedback.

7. The employer will provide relevant data to the Union as requested during the pilot. The
parties will meet in June 2026 for a mid-pilot review.
8. This MOU is a temporary agreement for the duration of the pilot program only and does not
establish precedent for future bargaining, nor does it alter the terms of the Collective
Bargaining Agreement except as specifically stated herein.
