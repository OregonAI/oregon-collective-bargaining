                                  Me m ora ndum of Agre e m e nt (MOA)
                                       By and Between
                Washington County Police Officer’s Association (“Association” or
                                         “WCPOA”)

                                                     And

                           Washington County Sheriff’s Office (“WCSO”)


RE: Washington County Jail In-s e rvic e Tra ining

The Washington County Police Officer’s Association (WCPOA) hereby agrees to allow the
Washington County Sheriff’s Office (WCSO) to schedule mandatory Washington County Jail in-
service training on jail division employee’s regularly scheduled days off, provided that the
shifts available at the spring rotation are based on the previously discussed 3 s hifts of 10 hours a
day.

The scope of this agreement shall be limited to staff whose primary duty assignment is the
Washington County Jail, commonly referred to by both parties as “line s taff” or “line deputies”.

In an effort to minimize the impact on the employee’s personal time and unless mutually agreed
upon by the employee and the WCSO, mandatory in-service training shall be scheduled on the
employee's regularly scheduled day off that is adjacent to either the first or last day of their work
week. Furthermore, in-service training shall not be scheduled on or directly adjacent to an
employee’s Guaranteed Vacation (GV).

The WCSO shall have the option to schedule a maximum of four (4) total in-service days per
calendar year (January 1 through December 31). In 2025, the WCSO shall have the option to schedule
a maximum of two (2) in-service days between January 1 - June 30.

All in-service time worked on an employee’s regularly scheduled day off shall be paid at time
and one-half, in accordance with the WCPOA Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA).

The initial agreement was on January 18, 2023, with an expiration date of March 1, 2024.
The second agreement was on December 26, 2023, with an expiration date of December 31, 2024.
The parties mutually agree to extend this agreement until June 30, 2025. Any qualified mandatory in-
service training that is initiated prior to midnight on June 30, 2025, remains eligible through the end of
the overtime training period.
AGREED to this date,____
                    12/4/2024
