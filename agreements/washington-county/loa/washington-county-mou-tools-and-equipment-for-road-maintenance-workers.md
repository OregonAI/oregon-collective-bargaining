                         Tools and Equipment for Road Maintenance Workers

                                                Schedule A

This is a representative list to illustrate the general type and nature of tools and equipment that are
typically used by the Road Maintenance Workers I. It is not intended to be exhaustive. New or different
tools and equipment are periodically needed to perform the work of the Road Maintenance Workers I.
This list will be updated at least annually but exclusion of a tool or piece of equipment at the time of
needing to use the resource does not preclude it from being eligible for management to assign its use.

Small Tools

    •   Work zone traffic control devices (portable signs, stands, paddles, cones, barrels, etc.)
    •   Hand-tools (i.e. - hammer, screwdriver, wrench, rod & eye level, etc.)
    •   Garden tools (i.e. – various shovels, rakes, hoe, pry bar, hand tamper, etc.)
    •   Carpentry tools (i.e. - squares, levels, plumb bobs, tape measure, etc.)
