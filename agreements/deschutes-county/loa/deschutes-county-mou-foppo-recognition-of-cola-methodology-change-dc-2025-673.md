        Deschutes County, hereinafter referred to as "the County” and the Federation of Oregon
        Parole and Probation Officers, hereinafter referred to as “FOPPO” agree to the following
        modification of Article 33 of the Collective Bargaining Agreement between the County and
        FOPPO, dated July 5, 2023, effective immediately upon execution of this Memorandum of
        Understanding (MOU):

        Article 33 will be amended as follows (amendments in bold italics):



        For the contract period of July 1, 2025 through june 30, 2026 the wage schedule shall be
        amended to reflect a COLA equal to the "average 12-month CPI percentage" from the
        twelve months of the CPI-U, West Region, Size Class B/C cities using each month's CPI 12-
        month percentage change from January 2024 to December 2024. The CP!% used for each
        month is the "12~month percent change" - meaning the month is compared (for the CPI
        index) to the same month from the previous year. The COLA shall have a minimum
        increase of not tess than one percent (1.0%) and a maximum increase of not more than
        four percent (4.0%). Appendix C shows the calculation methodology for an example period.

        Any dispute concerning this agreement will be resolved under the terms of Article 7,
        Grievance Procedure of the CBA. Except as specifically modified by this MOU, all terms and
        conditions of the CBA shall remain in full force and effect. To the extent any of the terms of
        this MOU conflict with those in the CBA the terms and condition of this MOU shall prevail
        for so long as it is in effect,
