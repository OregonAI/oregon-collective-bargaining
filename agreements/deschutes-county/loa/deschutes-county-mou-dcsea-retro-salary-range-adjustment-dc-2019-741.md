                    MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING BETWEEN
                   THE DESCHUTES COUNTY SHERIFF EMPLOYEES
                     ASSOCIATION AND DESCHUTES COUNTY AND
                          THE DESCHUTES COUNTY SHERIFF
                               Document No. 2019-741

         The Deschutes County Sheriff Employees Association, hereinafter referred to as
the Association, and Deschutes County and the Deschutes County Sheriff, hereinafter
referred to as the County,     agree to the following modification of Article 13, section B
and Exhibit A of their collective bargaining agreement.

1.       Effective upon signing below, consistent with the intent and desire of both the
County and the Association, and specific to the negotiations and bargaining of the
collective bargaining agreement (cba), dated July 1, 2019 — June 30, 2022, the
County and the Association each agree to waive application of the mandate in Article
13, section B and in Exhibit A that salary range adjustments become effective on the
first pay period following approval of the contract and receipt of all required
signatures. Instead, and with regard to the cba dated July 1, 2019 — June 30, 2022,
the County will process and pay all salary range adjustments with retroactive
application to July 1, 2019.


2.     The Association agrees that this action (retroactive salary range adjustment)
does not create past practice and will not at any time be introduced or argued as
such by the Association.

3.      Any dispute between the County and the Association as to the application,
interpretation, or meaning of any term of this Memorandum of Understanding shall be
subject to the Grievance Procedure set forth in Article 34 of the parties’ collective
bargaining agreement.
