                              -- Cost of Living Increase for Fiscal Year 22/23 --
                                           Document Number: 2022-390

     Deschutes County, hereinafter referred to as “the County,” and the International Union of Operating
     Engineers, Local 701 hereinafter referred to as “IUOE,” agree to the following modification of Article 14
     of the Collective Bargaining Agreement between the County and IUOE, dated July 18, 2018 ("CBA"),
     effective immediately upon execution of this Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU"):

     ARTICLE 14 - COMPENSATION, Section 14:01, paragraph six will be amended as follows (amendment in
     bold italics):

     For the contract period of July 1, 2022 through June 30, 2023 the salary schedule, Exhibit "B", as
     amended in the paragraph above, shall be amended to reflect a COLA equal to the Consumer Price
     index (CP!) percentage change from January 2021 to January 2022 with a minimum increase of not less
     than one point five percent (1.5%) and a maximum increase of not more than five point five percent
     (5.5%).



     Any dispute concerning this agreement will be resolved under the terms of Article 6 -
     Grievance Procedures, of the CBA.

     Except as specifically modified by this MOU, all terms and conditions of the CBA shall remain in full
     force and effect. To the extent any of the terms of this MOU conflict with those in the CBA, the terms
     and conditions of this MOU shall prevail for so long as it is in effect.
