            2    Section 1.1 Association Recognized.

            3             The County recognizes the Association as the sole and exclusive bargaining

            6             County excluding supervisory and confidential employees as defined in ORS

            9             Municipal Employees. All references to nurses in the agreement designate
           10             both sexes and whenever either gender is used it shall be construed to
           11             include both male and female nurses.

           14             a position with an established FTE of .50 or greater.

### 1                                ARTICLE 4 – ASSOCIATION RIGHTS
            2    Section 4.1 Dues Deduction.
            3             While this agreement is in effect, the County agrees to deduct the monthly
            4             membership dues from the pay of those nurses who individually request, in
            5             writing, such deductions, and to remit the aggregate thereof, to the
            6             Association, not later than the tenth (10th) day of the next succeeding month,
            7             together with an itemized statement thereof.
            8

           10             additions, deletions, or rate changes for the County to deduct from the
           11             nurse’s wages to pay dues, fees, and any other assessments or authorized

           14             The Association shall indemnify, defend and save the County and/or any of

            2    Section 21.1 Progressive and Corrective Discipline.

            6             result in a written record to the nurse’s personnel file. The County shall
            7             neither discipline nor discharge post-probationary nurses without just cause.

            9

           10             An action or failure to act as it relates to a nurse’s performance of assigned
           11             duties or conduct that is in violation of regulations or written policy. When
           12             establishing just cause, the County shall ensure due process, fairness and
           13             consistency.

           19             event the nurse fails to request removal in a timely manner, no reprimand
           20             may be considered after three (3) years if it qualifies for removal and shall be
           21             removed when discovered. No written record of disciplinary action shall be
